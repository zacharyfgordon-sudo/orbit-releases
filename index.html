Private by design

  • No servers. Your people, notes, and history live on your devices — Orbit runs no cloud that could see them.
  • Your devices talk directly. iPhone and Mac sync over your own network, end-to-end encrypted with keys only they hold.
  • Nothing leaves without you. On-device intelligence does the remembering; what you share is always your call.
