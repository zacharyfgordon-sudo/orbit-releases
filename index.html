Security ahead of the curve

Post-quantum encryption

Every sync between your devices is sealed with a hybrid of ML-KEM-768 — the NIST post-quantum standard — and Curve25519. Even a future quantum computer can't read what your devices say to each other.

1050 years to crack

Brute-forcing one of Orbit's 256-bit keys would take today's fastest supercomputer on the order of 1050 years. The universe is about 1.4×1010 years old.

Choose your level

Four security levels — Sealed, Guarded, Lockdown, and Blackout — let you decide exactly how strict Orbit runs, from everyday ease to nothing-in, nothing-out.

No servers, ever

Your people, notes, and history live on your devices. Orbit runs no cloud that could see them, and your devices talk directly — keys never leave your hands.
